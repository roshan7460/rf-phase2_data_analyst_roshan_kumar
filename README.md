---

##  Visualizations Created  

- Sales Distribution by Region  
- Total Sales by Region  
- Top 5 Customers by Sales  
- Total Profit by Category  
- Year-wise Sales Trend  

---

##  Key Business Insights  

- Central region contributes the highest total sales.  
- Office Supplies generates the highest total profit.  
- Sales show consistent year-over-year growth.  
- A small number of customers drive major revenue.  
- Some regions show strong sales but moderate profit margins.
